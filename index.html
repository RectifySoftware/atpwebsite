The Premise

An 18-year-old, a Menorca resort, and a boy he calls "Surfer."

Sam is on a ten-day family holiday in Menorca when he spots a boy at the resort on day two: blonde, long surfer-style hair, blue eyes, tall, athletic, probably Irish. They never speak. They just keep looking. Sam has three days left and one question worth asking: what if the looks actually mean something?

The Question

What if the looks actually mean something?

Sam has three working theories. None of them are confirmed. Vote for the one you believe (it changes nothing, but it's something to do while waiting for day seven).

Purely for fun, nothing here is canon, and it won't change how the story actually plays out.

Where We Are

Day six of ten. The eye-contact streak is real. Everything else is a theory.

Five days of glances, one accidental nod, and a lot of internal monologue. The story so far is confirmed to have happened, everything about what it means is still up for debate.
